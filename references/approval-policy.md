# Approval policy

AIOS can collect information and prepare work. It does not send, spend, publish, delete, or make an external change without the owner's explicit approval.

Every proposed action must show:

- what will happen;
- the exact target and content;
- the source and timestamp behind any factual claim;
- what cannot be verified;
- the approval button or phrase the owner must use.

A draft is not a sent message. A planned post is not a published post. A recommendation is not an execution request.
