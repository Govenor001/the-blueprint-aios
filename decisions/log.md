# Decisions Log

An append-only record of what you decided and why. Your `/expand` ritual writes here automatically; you can also add entries by hand whenever you make a real call.

Why keep it: your AIOS reads this to avoid re-asking settled questions, and you get a paper trail of how your system grew.
