# The 7-Day AIOS Setup Challenge

The platform is already built before the student arrives. The seven days are for connecting the student's own services, filling in their business context, approving safety boundaries, and proving the system works.

## Daily path

| Day | Student outcome | What the platform does |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Confirm Claude access, server, password, and optional services | Bootstrap, dashboard, model routing, and private folders are ready |
| 1 | Name the AIOS and complete the business interview | Onboard writes the context files required by installed skills |
| 2 | Approve the voice and content preferences | Content skills are already installed; the student reviews drafts |
| 3 | Connect the watchlist and verify a brief | Scout gathers sourced signals and records timestamps |
| 4 | Review a newsletter and approval flow | Operator prepares drafts and never sends without approval |
| 5 | Connect Google Workspace or another service | Composio and the OpenAPI bridge are ready for read-only checks |
| 6 | Connect Telegram and optional voice | The owner can ask questions from the phone |
| 7 | Run the acceptance checklist | Metrics, activity, source tracking, and schedules are verified |

## What students receive

- A prebuilt Mission Control dashboard with the map, panels, activity feed, and authenticated API.
- The built-in AIOS workflows plus the curated Business OS 150-skill manifest and installer.
- Scout, Operator, and Advisor safety contracts.
- Private vault ingestion, keyword/local-model search, model-tier routing, connectors, and timers.
- A one-command Ubuntu deployment path and a student acceptance checklist.

Students do not build the platform from scratch. They connect their own accounts, add their own files and context, review drafts, and confirm that each day produces the expected result.

Start with [00-SERVICES-AND-SETUP.md](00-SERVICES-AND-SETUP.md), then follow the relevant day file. Use [ACCEPTANCE.md](ACCEPTANCE.md) on Day 7.
