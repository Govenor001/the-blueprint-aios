# Day 1 — Lay the Foundation

*Floor 1 of The Blueprint. Today your AI stops being a stranger and learns who you are. No wiring, no automation yet — just the Foundation everything else stands on. ~20 minutes.*

**By the end of Day 1:** a named AIOS that knows your business, your voice, and your priorities — and can tell you what to focus on this week, in your own words.

---

## Why this comes first

The mistake almost everyone makes with AI is asking it to *do* things before it *knows* anything. You'd never hand a new hire a task list on day one without telling them what the company does. Today we give your AI that context, once — and it compounds forever.

## Step 1 — Get the kit onto your machine

Clone this repo into a working folder (or download and unzip it). Then, in your terminal:

```
cd the-blueprint-aios
claude
```

## Step 2 — Run the one-paste install

Paste this into Claude Code exactly:

> Read `INSTALL.md`, then set me up. Confirm the four core skills in `.claude/skills/` are present, read `references/the-blueprint-framework.md` and `references/the-architects-loop.md` so you understand the system, then run the `/blueprint` skill to onboard me.

Claude reads its own instructions, checks the kit, learns the two frameworks, and starts your onboarding interview.

## Step 3 — Answer the 7 questions honestly

The `/blueprint` interview asks seven things. Answer as *you*, about *your* business:

1. Who you are, what you sell, who you sell it to.
2. **Paste** one or two things you've actually written (an email, a post) — don't type fresh ones. This is how it learns your real voice.
3. Your 2–3 biggest priorities for the next 90 days.
4. Where your revenue lands and how it's tracked.
5. Where you talk to customers and your team.
6. Where your notes and docs live.
7. The one task that eats your week.

> **The voice rule matters.** Paste real writing. If you type polished prose for the AI, it learns a fake version of your voice. Copy from a sent email or a real post.

## Step 4 — Name it and give it a personality

It'll ask what to call your AIOS and what personality it should have — dry and efficient, warm and chatty, whatever fits you. **Do this properly.** Naming it is what turns "an AI tool" into *your* assistant. Ours is Jarvis; yours is yours.

## Step 5 — The wow moment

When onboarding finishes, ask it:

> [Name], what should I focus on this week?

It answers using only what it just learned — in your voice, tied to your real priorities. That's the Foundation proving itself. If the answer feels generic, your Q2 voice samples or Q3 priorities were thin — re-run `/blueprint` and give it more.

---

## Adapt-to-you note

Everything today is *your* input. Two people finishing Day 1 have completely different AIOS's — one might be a real-estate wholesaler's, one a fitness coach's. Same kit, same steps, entirely different brain. That's the point: we teach the *how*; the *what* is yours.

## Done when

A fresh question — "what does my business do and who am I?" — gets answered correctly, in your voice, with no extra prompting.

## Proof of build

Post your AIOS's **name** and its **answer to "what should I focus on this week?"** Everyone's is different — that's what makes the thread fun, and it's proof your Foundation is real.
