# The Skill Vault — 10 workshops to install as you grow

The four core skills (`/blueprint`, `/inspect`, `/expand`, `/rescue`) run your AIOS. The Vault is ten pre-built **workshops** — real jobs, one per wing — that you install and personalize instead of building from scratch. Copy a folder into `.claude/skills/`, run `/blueprint` context through it, and it's yours.

Each is stamped Scaffolding Phase 1: it runs manually until you trust it, then you advance it.

| Skill | Wing | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `brief` | Intelligence | Your morning brief: overnight news in your niche, ranked, with your take |
| `content-week` | Content | A week of posts + carousel outlines in your voice, from one prompt |
| `newsletter` | Content | Turns your brief into a send-ready newsletter |
| `watch` | Intelligence | Watches named competitors/keywords and pings you when something moves |
| `qualify` | Growth | Scores a lead list and tells you who to call first and why |
| `outreach` | Growth | Drafts personalized first-touch messages from a lead list |
| `followup` | Growth | Runs a timed follow-up sequence so nobody falls through |
| `inbox` | Comms | Triages your inbox: what needs a reply, what can wait, drafts the replies |
| `plan-day` | Comms | Reads calendar + tasks and builds your day around your priorities |
| `remember` | Foundation | Saves a fact to memory so your AIOS stops asking twice |

**Install order that works:** `brief` and `content-week` first (fastest wow), then one Growth skill, then `inbox`/`plan-day` once your Comms Wing is wired.

All ten are built and ready — copy the folder into `.claude/skills/`, feed it your context, and it's yours. Each is stamped Scaffolding Phase 1, so it runs manually until you trust it. When you outgrow them, author your own the same way with `/expand`.
